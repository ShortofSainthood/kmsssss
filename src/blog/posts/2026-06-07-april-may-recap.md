## Puzzles

### 4x4 Rubik's Cube

Me and some friends took a trip to our local mall towards the beginning of April. We stopped at Barnes and Noble's, and they had a whole section dedicated to twisty puzzles! I bought myself a 4x4 Rubik's Cube since I've gotten kind of bored with my 3x3. I also got myself a cute taba squishy from Claire's.

<figure class="center">
  <img class="third" src="/assets/images/2026-06-04-mall-haul.jpeg" alt="A 4x4 Rubik's Cube still in it's box and a pink and blue star-shaped taba squishy on a table.">
  <figcaption>
    <p>
      My mini mall "haul".
    </p>
  </figcaption>
</figure>

I got so excited that I immediately started scrambling and solving it at the restaurant we were at. I was able to solve the white side the way you would a 3x3, but couldn't for the life of me figure out the other sides. I especially struggled with keeping things organized on the opposite side of whatever side I was working on.

I really wanted to figure it out without a guide, but after weeks of messing around with no progress, I eventually looked up a tutorial. After reading a [tutorial that uses the reduction method](http://www.speedcube.us/blogs/speedcubing-solutions/how-to-solve-a-4x4-rubiks-cube-complete-beginners-guide), I felt silly for not thinking of that method before. It's not as hard as I thought it'd be. It's basically just solving a 3x3 with a few extra steps. The hardest part for me is memorizing the algorithms.

<img src="/assets/images/2026-06-04-solved-4x4.jpeg" alt="Me holding up my solved 4x4 Rubik's cube.">

I probably should've taken a photo of the cube while it was scrambled because these photos back-to-back make it look like all I did was take it out of the box. Oh well.

### Listening To

#### Olivia Rodrigo - The Cure

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B402rKl4bUg

Olivia Rodrigo's releasing a new album later this month and I've enjoyed both singles she's put out so far. I especially enjoy her latest release, The Cure. I love how the song builds as it goes on, especially how the drums kick in on the bridge. 

I also find the lyrics to be pretty relatable and layered. I can strongly relate to the feeling of wanting someone to swoop in and "fix you", but feeling guilty when they can't, or having things in your life technically be going well but still being absolutely miserable.

#### PinkPantheress - Girl Like Me

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qRAJowgHqxA

PinkPantheress also come out with a new single a little while ago, Girl Like Me.

I *adore* the music video, I can't help but smile every time I watch it. Not only does it capture the era so accurately, but it expands on it in a fun and interesting way. PinkPantheress is doing more than just going "hey, remember 2007? That sure was cool right?" like so many other people using the "Y2K aesthetic" do.

Some people in the comments have mentioned her being inspired by the Rhythm Heaven series, one of my favorite video game series. I'm not a big enough PinkPantheress fan to know if this is true or not, but it wouldn't surprise me. The part with the guy waving his tennis racket around reminds me of [Rhythm Rally](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hzhcBSjhoCI).
